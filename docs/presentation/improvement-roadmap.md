## Before submission

### The visitor understands the world

Keep the first playable whisper visible long enough to read: every flower is a memory, and shared people or places create threads. Make the fictional attribution clear. Give planting and seasonal advance visible word actions, without requiring the hidden debug slider.

Acceptance: three unfamiliar visitors can explain flowers, threads, and tending after a short visit. Fix any explanation two people miss before adding features. The owner runs these sessions; do not mark them complete without participants.

### The visitor cares before something fades

Present Grandma Mei's original quotation and the vivid rooftop before the seasonal transition. Keep their positions recognizable. Frame the dissolve, leave four seconds of quiet, and preserve the seed. Verify that a visitor who preserves the rooftop gets the corresponding truthful ending.

Acceptance: all four approved action branches pass engine tests, and the film depicts its actual branch. No cinematic cue forces a different outcome.

### The new memory changes something visible

Keep the two authored choices distinct. Tea deepens the family region. The rooftop fragment links a place to Leo. The selected fragment's preview must match the actual resulting threads, and the first thread must finish before the next beat.

Acceptance: a visitor can point to the new flower and describe what it joined. Keep all new copies of a chosen memory under a unique stable identity.

### The final garden has a readable shape

Show color, outlines, and quiet spaces together before emphasizing a surviving relationship group. Limit labels to a few readable phrases. Keep the closing caption away from flowers and controls. Do not make every lost seed disappear to improve the screenshot.

Acceptance: the ending remains legible at the real recording resolution and as a listing thumbnail. Take both a full-resolution screenshot and a small preview before choosing the listing image.

### The audience can try and vote

Use the verified deployed playable URL in the submission. Put a direct voting link beside it wherever the contest platform permits. Test both links in a fresh browser session. Keep the presentation's request to vote simple and outside the in-garden loss scene.

Acceptance: a person with no context can open the site, perform a meaningful action, and find the contest voting destination. Do not invent a vote URL while the organizers' destination is unknown.

### The presentation survives a failed live demo

Keep the 90 second recording downloaded locally. Use the same opening and closing around either the live route or the recording. A muted version needs readable captions. Keep a screenshot of the final composition available separately.

Acceptance: rehearse the live and recorded routes with a timer. Verify the video's actual duration and the spoken pace. The recording and speaker checks require the owner.

## After the contest

### Make the first personal garden rewarding

Reuse thread previews in first plantings. Show a quiet hint when no relationship appears. Let visitors correct a person or place before it becomes a thread. Preserve private local storage and make optional provider use clear.

Acceptance: a visitor plants several genuine memories and understands at least one real connection without using the fictional demo as an explanation.

### Make meaningful connections easier to express

Add explicit entity correction, then a manual connection action that asks the visitor what the two memories share. Keep manual links distinguishable in the data model. Do not fabricate relationships or encourage repetitive text to maximize survival.

Acceptance: corrections survive reload and do not reappear incorrectly after a later tagging answer. Manual connections respect the existing engine rules and return behavior.

### Extend the authored life into chapters

Use the approved three-chapter outline only after the single season feels complete. Add a chapter for earlier family memories, a chapter for leaving and friendship, and a final reflection. Keep intentions optional and resource grants explicit within the fictional mode.

Acceptance: each chapter introduces a distinct decision and visible consequence. Personal gardens keep their existing real-time rules unless the owner separately changes that product direction.

### Let visitors share the world's shape

Add a local garden portrait export with memory text hidden by default. Offer an explicit choice before including words or photographs. A shared still should express relationships without exposing personal content accidentally.

Acceptance: the default image contains no personal text or source photographs. Export operates locally and remains independent of accounts or public hosting.
