# Decisions

Where the project plan, the screens and the design system disagreed, this file says which one wins. Newest at the top. If you change a decision, edit it here first, then the code.

| # | Topic | Decision | Why | Overrides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D27 | Music and louder sound | (1) **The garden has music**: a slow, bittersweet waltz in C (`src/sound/score.ts`, played by `src/sound/sound.ts` on its own `Tone.Clock`), made only of chords that hold the flowers' pentatonic, so any hover lands in tune. A clear garden plays IV–I–vi–V; from a fog share of 0.35 the next round turns to vi–IV–ii–iii, the lowpass closes and the melody thins. It steps back to 35% while a season's losses play or the film holds still. (2) **More moments sound:** opening a memory, going back, opening planting, a season passing (wind), what holds you, the film's title cards, light gained (systems.md § Sound). (3) **Levels raised for laptop speakers.** Measured at the output, `?demo`, production build: music with drone idle peak −21.5 dB (rms −29.7); hover −14 to −15.6 (rms −22, about 8 dB over the music); opening a memory −18; tend with ripple −10.6; planting with its chord −14.7; what holds you −17.4; a 21-day return with wind and losses −11.1. Limiter ceiling −3 (was −6). | Owner, 13 Oct: "there is only a sound of getting into the website but nothing else", asking for "happy yet kinda sad music" and sounds on hover. The drone is 65 Hz, below what laptop speakers play, and the hover note peaked at −24 dB, so on a laptop the garden sounded silent after its entry cascade. The pentatonic melody over IV–I–vi–V is the bittersweet the owner asked for and keeps pillar 1: dusk, never failure. | systems.md's sound table (music added); P11.3's headphone levels. |
| D26 | The three-minute film, its words and its colour | (1) **The film is the whole demo, about 3:00 in seven acts** (`src/ui/story.ts`, `FILM_SECONDS` 180): prologue, fragments, growing, choosing, a season, another choice, coda. Its second season regrows the same life in the same film (`rewind()`: `beginEpisode('film')` with the film's story kept and its generation guard moved on) and plants the rooftop fragment instead of the tea, with the light still on Grandma Mei: the engine keeps the rooftop as a ghost (0.12) through its new thread alone, where the first season lost it. Every outcome is the engine's own; nothing is forced lost or brought back. (2) **Words a room can read.** Episode captions use the design system's memory type, `memory-lg` (32px) while the film plays and `memory` (24px) in play, in `text-primary`, each fading in over 1.2s (blur 6px → 0). Six **title cards** (`FILM_TITLES`, sentence case like the threshold's "A life becomes a garden.") sit in the empty sky at 15% in the `title` style (40px Cormorant Light), fading in over 1.8s. A soft band of night (`rgba(11,14,20,0.66)`, no edge) sits behind each line so a thread crossing it never cuts a letter (a full-brightness thread reads 95–155 inside it, the letters about 230). (3) **Colour is life.** Living flowers let motes of their own colour rise (`src/scene/fx/Pollen.tsx`: vivid 7, fading 3, ghost 0; alpha 0.55 / 0.3; rise 1.1 over 7–11s; one additive point cloud, one draw call, rebuilt only when a state changes; dimmed to 35% while a memory is open, off under reduced motion). Cluster pools on the ground take their flowers' colour (`uSpotColor`: vitality-weighted `flowerColor` of the living members, mixed halfway to fog; glow 0.06 × lift + 0.035 × ring), so a forgotten corner goes grey with its flowers. | Owner, 13 Oct: "messages can be a little bigger because you cant see them", more of the design colours, and a three-minute demo that is "really impressive showing the main points of the project and making it philosophical". Showing two choices in one film proves the second pillar without a live step on stage. Pollen and pools use only `flowerColor` (the kind tokens), never a thread. Measured on the AMD 610M, production build: median frame 1–8 ms through the whole film; the rare 50–600 ms stalls move between runs (shared iGPU), as in E5. | Design system Whisper (14px fog text) for the episode only; D24's "90-second film"; the look freeze (B1) for these three additions. |
| D25 | Episode presentation | (1) **Tending shows its reach:** in the episode, an open living memory with light to spend keeps the memories `tend()` would spill light into (the engine's `neighbours`, `useTendReach`) at 85% instead of 30%, and lights the threads between them (×1.4). (2) **The closing composition lights gently:** a presented holder's group at full strength, the rest of the changed garden at 45% (threads 40%), so colour, outlines and seeds all stay part of the picture; the reveal keeps its 15%. (3) **The rooftop stands at (1290, 760)** and the rooftop fragment plants at (1140, 720): at (1370, 750) the rooftop sat within ~20 canvas units of the home view's edge, so its seed was hard to find. (4) **Reduced motion cuts the camera** to its goal instead of gliding. (5) **The episode is prepared behind the threshold** (`beginEpisode(mode, prepareMs)`, then `openEpisode`), so the threshold never fades onto the reference garden and replaces it. | Owner's plan §2 ("highlight the actual living neighbors"), §4 ("gently emphasize… keep dark seeds visible"), §2 layout ("keep the rooftop visible and easy to select") and §6 (reduced motion). | D24 details; reveal dimming (D20/P9) in the episode ending only |
| D24 | Contest episode | **23 authored starting memories + one optional planting, one remaining light, and one explicit 21-garden-day season.** The episode clock pauses between actions. Episode data never overwrites a personal garden. | Owner's approved implementation plan, 13 Oct. | D1 | Rendering | **3D: Three.js through React Three Fiber, drei and postprocessing.** Flowers are procedural meshes that turn to face the camera around their own vertical axis (Y-billboard), so they read like the 2D screens while living in real depth, fog and parallax. | D2 | Forgetting numbers | **Design system v2:** S₀ = 7 garden days, tend multiplies S by 1.8, states vivid ≥ 0.6, fading ≥ 0.3, ghost ≥ 0.08, lost below. | Newer, and the components already use these thresholds. | Plan (S=10, ×1.5, 0.7/0.4/0.15) |
| D3 | Thread bonus | Each thread adds 5 × weight to S, **saturating at 8 days** (`THREAD_BONUS_CAP`). | `npm run sim` showed uncapped bonuses make a well-connected garden almost immortal (23 of 25 survived with no tending). With the cap, a neglected garden loses about half, and the survivors are the connected ones. | Plan ("each thread adds 5") |
| D4 | Is loss permanent? | **No. A lost memory leaves a small dark seed and keeps its words.** Returning costs 1 light and brings it back as a ghost (vitality 0.2, fresh stability). Its threads re-form. | The design system says "a lost memory can always be returned to", and so does the dissolve storyboard's last frame. Gentler loss fits pillar 1. | Plan's suggested answer ("text is gone") |
| D5 | Letting go | `let go` is the person's choice: the memory drifts to lost immediately, with no confirmation and no red. Same return rule as D4. | Design system Button rules. | — |

| D13 | Tagging | **Offline first.** The browser's offline tagger (compromise NLP + relation words + a feelings word list) tags every memory at planting. A provider is an optional upgrade, chosen by which key the server has: Claude `claude-haiku-5-5` (`effort: "low"`, structured output) if `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is set, else Groq `openai/gpt-oss-20b` (strict JSON schema, free tier) if `GROQ_API_KEY` is set, else 503. A provider's tags replace the offline ones when they arrive. The client stops asking after a 503, waits 3 minutes (or `retry-after`) after a 429, and gives up after 4s (raised from 2.5s on 13 Oct: a provider's first answer after a cold start took 2.6s; planting never waits for it either way). | Lethe is a demo without a paid key. The garden must connect memories with no key at all, planting must never wait on the network, and a free tier must never be hammered. Haiku is plenty for a small extraction task. | Earlier D13 (Claude Opus only; offline matched known names only) |

| D23 | Camera exploration | **The camera is the person's.** Drag to turn (azimuth ±70°), scroll to come close or rise above (distance 3–24, zooming toward the cursor, polar floor 30°), right-drag or two fingers to move over the garden (the look-at point stays clamped over it), <kbd>WASD</kbd>/arrows to glide, <kbd>Q</kbd>/<kbd>E</kbd> to turn. Focus and the reveal remember where the person was and glide back afterwards, unless they moved mid-goal; any touch hands the camera back at once. Idle drift is a small additive offset, never a pull back. | A garden is walked through, not orbited at a fixed post; the old clamps (±25°, 55–83°, no pan) fought exploring and the focus glide. Gestures are never fought (pillar: tenderness over control). | scene-spec § World limits, P1–P3 review camera clamps |
| D22 | What a fading memory forgets | **The specifics go first**, as in real forgetting: the memory's own people and places, other names and numbers, then other words that carry meaning, and the small words that hold a sentence together last (`src/ui/forgetWords.ts`). Shares: fading 15% (specifics only), ghost 45%, lost 85%; at least one word always stays; a name of several words goes as a whole; quote marks and punctuation stay. Seeded per memory, and each state forgets everything the one before it forgot. "held by" is shown **only while vivid**, and lists only people and places shared with another living memory (its threads), plus the year. | Words used to drop at random, so a ghost often lost "the" and "to" but kept every name, and "held by Grandma Mei · Hangzhou · 2014" under it spelled out the rest: a faded memory stayed easy to read (owner, 13 Oct). Now a ghost keeps its shape but not what made it particular: "⋯ ⋯ kitchen in ⋯, smelling of osmanthus". What holds it is still shown by its lit threads on hover, and by the whisper when it is at risk. | Design system Recollection (random drops from ghost on; held-by always shown) |
| D21 | Words falling away | When a memory has lost words, each dropped word leaves through its gap: it blurs (0 → 3px), sinks 0.55em and fades over 2.8s, starting 0.6s in and 0.18s after the previous word; then only the design system's dotted gap remains. Reduced motion: no animation. | The first pillar made visible on every hover of a fading memory: "first their colour, then their words". Zero GPU cost. | `lethe-design-system` Recollection markup (one inner span added) |

| D19 | Threads in a big garden | Up to **24 threads, every thread is drawn** (the demo garden has 8).
